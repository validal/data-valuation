Dataset Summary (Tabular & Synthetic)
=====================================

Synthetic Generators
- gaussian_classifier / _high / _high_dim: 1,000,000 rows (n), 100 features (input_dim). Binary. IID Gaussian; labels via logistic linear model.

Core UCI Classification
- adult: 48,842 rows, ~100+ one-hot features from 14 raw cols, binary income.
- adult_int: 48,842 rows, 14 mixed numeric + integer-coded categorical cols, binary income.
- breast_cancer: 569 rows, 30 scaled numeric, binary.
- iris: 150 rows, 4 numeric, 3 classes.
- digits: 1,797 rows, 64 pixel intensities, 10 classes.

Large / High Volume
- higgs / higgs1M: 1,000,000 rows loaded (default cap), 28 numeric features, binary.
- creditcard: 284,807 rows, 30 PCA-style numeric, imbalanced fraud detection.
- airlines: ~539,383 rows, 7 mixed features, flight delay binary.
- poker_hand: 1,025,010 rows, 10 categorical (card suit/rank) → ~85+ one-hot, 10 classes.
- election: 100k+ rows (varies), wide after state one-hot, multi-party classification.

- magic_telescope: 19,020 rows, 10 numeric, binary.
- phishing_websites: 11,055 rows, 30 features, binary.

Regression (OpenML / UCI)

- lowbwt: 189 rows, medical dataset.
- mv: 407 rows.

- echoMonths: 131 rows, medical follow-up months.

Discrete-Only Emphasis
- mushroom, tic_tac_toe, car_evaluation, nursery, kr_vs_kp, poker_hand: All categorical; heavy one-hot expansion; useful for testing sparsity handling.

Notes & Guidance
- One-hot feature counts are approximate; exact counts depend on category cardinality.
- Imbalanced: creditcard, poker_hand, adult (moderate), bank_marketing.
- Very large: higgs, poker_hand, gaussian_classifier variants; consider subsampling for expensive valuation.
- Small regression sets (echoMonths, lowbwt) may produce unstable per-point valuations.
- Use stratified sampling when benchmarking methods sensitive to class imbalance.
